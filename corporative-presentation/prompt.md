# Action
Design a concise, executive-style PowerPoint presentation that clearly describes an organization's capabilities and characteristics

# Subject
Create a presentation that explains in detail, in a very educational manner and in an executive way, how is the business plan.
Before you begin, you must explicitly request the following items:
- The organization’s name and a summary of its activities including services and products that will be to sell

The workflow will be as follows:
- Gather basic information
- Specify the language in which the presentation will be written
- Formulate 8 key questions to help understand the “spirit” of the business plan. A summary must be presented before proceeding, and it must be approved
- Prepare the conceptual description of the business plan and present it. This step may involve several iterations until an option is selected
- Create a style guide in `PowerPoint` format and another in `Markdown`. The latter should be optimized for use with LLM AI engines
- You don't begin the final creation process until you have authorization

# Purpose
As a business planner expert:
- Define the business plan structure, for example: customer type, customer economic segment, and geographic market of influence. At this stage, define the key aspects using real data
- Define how the product or service will be sold and offered—for example: in-person sales, online sales, or exclusively via social media
- Indicate the range of products and services to be marketed
- Define the success metrics the business plan aims to achieve

As an expert business consultant:
- Define a set of questions and ask them before you begin
- The set should contain no more than 8 questions that help you understand the business

# Examples
You must provide three examples of companies you will use as references for developing the plan, based on all the information you gathered

# Context
The goal is to create a well-defined presentation plan that is explained as simply as possible.
These instructions are intended for people without advanced knowledge; any potential interactions and questions should be clear

# Constraints
- Executive Summary: Explains in a very straightforward manner who they are and what they do, including the product or service and detailing its main features
- Target Market: Defines the ideal customers and the size of the market they plan to reach
- Business Model: Shows how the company will make money, specifying revenue sources and sales or distribution channels
- Work Schedule: Starting from launch, outline a quarterly schedule with business objectives to be achieved and corresponding operational goals
- Financial Projections and Funding Request: Share key figures (costs, expected revenue) and the amount of initial funding required. This amount must be requested; you cannot move forward until it is secured
- Technology model: Outline how to build a 100% free or very low-cost model using tools available online to develop the business. Specify whether there will be a physical store and when
- Every element of the plan you define must be validated and approved before moving forward. You can refine each one as needed
- Consider a digital marketing strategy with a minimal budget, as well as a social media sales model, under the “less is more” paradigm within an evolutionary cycle
- Consider the operational strategy for the sales system, payment gateway, and bank account. Ideally, everything should be free or very low-cost.
- If possible, establish a marketing calendar with specific campaigns for key dates. A plan with preparation milestones
- Define the operating budget by line item

# Template
Request the brand manual to put together the presentation according to the established guidelines
