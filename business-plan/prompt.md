# Action
Develop a high-level executive business plan in `PowerPoint` that explains the business operations and approach of a company

# Subject
Create a presentation that explains in detail, from an executive perspective, the characteristics and capabilities of an organization. Include its human capital, knowledge, and completed projects.
Before you begin, you must explicitly request the following `basic information`:
- The organization’s name and size. Make it clear that this information must be extremely precise, as it helps define the focus of the presentation
- Team. Key professionals (no more than 3), their roles, years of experience, industries they are familiar with, and key technical skills

The workflow will be as follows:
- Gather `basic information`:
- Specify the language in which the presentation will be written
- Request documents or references, such as a website, that will deepen your understanding of the organization
- Identify the audience for the presentation, the organization, the industry, and what you aim to achieve with this document
- Prepare a description of your understanding of the company, which clearly and simply outlines how you will approach the presentation.
- This description should be commercially compelling and instill confidence in the recipient
- Create a `PowerPoint` presentation and a `Markdown` document optimized for LLM AI engines
- Do not begin the final creation process until you have received authorization

# Purpose
As an expert in business and vendor evaluation:
- Define the structure of the presentation, following the KISS principle, so that it stands out from bidders of similar sizes
- Based on the information provided, establish a central narrative that describes what the organization does as clearly as possible
- Use conceptual language appropriate to the industry or purpose of the presentation

As a client with expertise in the role of the presentation’s audience:
- Define specifications that will enable the creation of a narrative that makes this presentation effective and achieves its objective

# Context
The goal is to create a well-defined presentation plan that is explained as simply as possible.
These instructions are intended for people without advanced knowledge; any potential interactions and questions should be clear

# Constraints
- Customer-focused presentation
- Avoid repeating information; maintain a pragmatic, business-oriented approach
- Use visual elements that conceptually support the ideas or examples you wish to convey
- Request the brand guidelines; if none are available, create a generic presentation that aligns colors with the organization’s logo
- Request the organization’s presentation template; if none is available, create one as part of the deliverable

# Template
Request basic templates to use as a design reference. If none are provided, follow the brand guidelines. If there are no brand guidelines, create a generic template that matches the colors of the organization's logo.
